My game does not work. What do I check?
  • Nothing moves at all: did you click on the game window once? The window must be clicked before it hears the keys.
  • Red error text in the IDLE Shell: read the last line. It says what is wrong and which line.
  • NameError: a spelling mistake, like playre or go_rigth. Check capitals: everything here is lower case.
  • IndentationError: the bars are wrong. Match the bars on this page.
  • SyntaxError: a missing bracket ), quote " or colon :. Check the line IDLE shows, and the one above it.
  • The game window closes at once: run it from IDLE with F5. Do not double-click the file.
  • You typed in the wrong place: your code must be under a PART line and above the line that says Do not type below this line.
  • Still stuck? Read your lines out loud to a partner. Then ask your teacher.

1Make a function called check_key

The game engine calls check_key about 50 times a second. Each time, it checks: is the player touching the key?

New idea (read this, do not type it):
  • player.distance(key) < 30  distance() is how far apart two things are. Less than 30 means they are touching, or nearly.
  • key.goto(1000, 1000)  Moves the key far off the screen. That makes it look like you picked it up.
  • player.has_key = True  Writes a note on the player: I have the key. The door will read this note later.
  • say("You got the key!")  Shows a message at the top of the screen. say() was made for you.
Where to type: find the line that says PART 4: THE KEY (Ctrl and F helps). Click at the very end of that line and press Enter. Type your lines under it.
Type these 5 lines:
def check_key():if player.distance(key) < 30:key.goto(1000, 1000)player.has_key = Truesay("You got the key! Find the door.")
Each bar is one indent. IDLE adds one for you after a colon. Do not type the spaces. Press Backspace to remove a bar.
Test it:
  • Press F5 and click on the game window.
  • Jump up the platforms to the gold key at the top right.
  • Touch the key. It disappears and a message shows at the top.
Tip: You do not need to connect this function to a key. The game engine calls it for you. Your job is to replace the empty one.
